server. It is a complete product, not a trial: there is no limit on servers
or VMs.

## Cloud and Business

Prices in euros, excluding VAT. Users are unlimited on every plan, and
there is no minimum term.

| Plan       | Where it runs     | Price                              | Size                                    |
| ---------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Business   | Your own servers  | 1,490 € per year, per installation | Unlimited servers and VMs               |
| Starter    | Cloud (we run it) | 29 € per month                     | Up to 25 servers and VMs                |
| Team       | Cloud (we run it) | 79 € per month                     | Up to 100 servers and VMs               |
| Scale      | Cloud (we run it) | 179 € per month                    | Up to 250 servers and VMs               |
| Enterprise | Cloud or your own | Quoted                             | More than 250, or agreed service levels |

- **InfraMole Business** — everything in Community, several workspaces (one
  per client or department), a signed offline licence (no phone-home), email
  support with a reply within two business days, help with the
  installation, and a commercial licence as an alternative to the AGPL.
- **InfraMole Cloud** — we run and maintain InfraMole for you, in the EU.
  Plans are sized by servers and VMs only; applications, databases,
  containers, domains and discovered services stay unlimited. Change
  history: 30, 90 or 365 days. Every Cloud workspace starts with 14 days of
  Team, free.
- **How to buy**: write to [sales@inframole.com](mailto:sales@inframole.com)
  with the plan and roughly how many servers you have. Everything is
  arranged by email and billed by invoice — monthly, or yearly with two
  months free. There is no online payment.
