# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 29 September 2026_

This policy covers **inframole.com** — the website, the documentation and
the live demo of InfraMole, a non-commercial open source project.

**Who is responsible:** Alejandro Galisteo (private individual). Contact for
anything related to your data:
[privacy@inframole.com](mailto:privacy@inframole.com).

If you run InfraMole on your own server, **none of your data reaches us**:
the software does not phone home. This policy is only about this website.

## What we process, why and for how long

| When                                           | Data                                                                        | Why                                                                                                   | How long                                                                               |
| ---------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You visit the website                          | IP address, browser and the pages requested, in the server's technical logs | To deliver the website and protect it against abuse (for example rate limiting) — legitimate interest | Rotated automatically by size (a few megabytes), so older entries are overwritten      |
| You open the live demo                         | A session cookie; the session records your IP address and browser           | To keep you signed in to the demo — necessary to provide it                                           | Until the session expires, 14 days after your last visit; expired sessions are deleted |
| You email us or use the in-app Feedback button | Your email address, name if given, and your message                         | To answer you — legitimate interest                                                                   | As long as needed for the conversation                                                 |

We do not create profiles, do not sell data and do not use analytics or
advertising trackers.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restrict or
object to its processing, and ask for a copy in a portable format, by writing
to [privacy@inframole.com](mailto:privacy@inframole.com). You can also
complain to the Spanish data protection authority,
[AEPD](https://www.aepd.es).

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with its date.
When InfraMole starts offering paid services, this policy will be extended
accordingly.
